About the school

A co-education O and A Level day and mixed boarding school in Namungoona, Kampala, registered by the Ministry of Education and Sports.

Preamble

Quality education, and a trade

To provide quality education at affordable fees. But most importantly to train learners in practical subjects that make them job creators.

At KCS we train the three domains: The Heart, Head and Hands. It is a co-education O and A Level Day and Mixed Boarding School, registered by the Ministry of Education and Sports.

Vision

To become a giant leading Education Institution and Social-Economic Development Centre.

Mission

To train God-fearing, Responsible, Self-Reliant and Corrupt-Free Citizens.

The three domains

The Heart, Head and Hands

Three things a student should leave with. The school describes its whole approach this way, and everything else follows from it.

  • The Heart

    Character first. We are committed to training God-fearing, responsible and corrupt-free citizens.

  • The Head

    The full national curriculum at both O and A Level, taught by experienced teachers with ICT in the classroom.

  • The Hands

    A practical trade learned properly and certified by the Directorate of Industrial Training.

Why families choose us

Why choose Kawaala College School

  • Modern teaching methods

    We use modern teaching methods and approaches, with ICT integrated into the classroom rather than kept for a separate lesson.

  • Uninterrupted learning

    Classrooms are backed by a standby generator, so lessons and practicals carry on whatever happens to the power.

  • Hands-on skills, certified

    Students get real hands-on experience and are awarded DIT certificates on completion of their course.

  • A balanced diet

    Students are fed on a balanced diet, prepared on site in the school kitchen and eaten in the dining hall.

  • Highly trained teachers

    Our teachers are highly trained and experienced, and they know the national curriculum inside out.

  • Affordable fees and bursaries

    Affordable school fees, plus bursary programmes from PESS and other organisations for families who need them.

Above all, KCS is committed to training God-fearing students.
