Welcome to KCS

A school that trains job creators

Kawaala College School is a mixed day and boarding secondary school in Namungoona, on the western edge of Kampala. We teach the full O and A Level curriculum, and we teach it alongside a trade. Students sit their national papers at our own UNEB centre.

What sets the school apart is what happens after the bell. A student can leave here holding an A Level certificate and a DIT certificate in tailoring, catering or motor vehicle mechanics. That is the whole point of the place: we are training job creators, not job seekers.

  • Registered by the Ministry of Education and Sports
  • UNEB examination centre U1314
  • DIT vocational centre UVQF/5176
  • Affordable fees, with PESS and other bursaries

The three domains

The Heart, Head and Hands

At KCS we train all three. It is the school's own way of describing what a complete education looks like, and everything below follows from it.

  • The Heart

    Character before anything else. We are committed to training God-fearing, responsible and corrupt-free citizens.

  • The Head

    The full national curriculum at both O and A Level, taught by experienced teachers with ICT in the classroom.

  • The Hands

    A practical trade learned properly and certified by the Directorate of Industrial Training.

Why families choose us

Why choose Kawaala College School

  • Modern teaching methods

    We use modern teaching methods and approaches, with ICT integrated into the classroom rather than kept for a separate lesson.

  • Uninterrupted learning

    Classrooms are backed by a standby generator, so lessons and practicals carry on whatever happens to the power.

  • Hands-on skills, certified

    Students get real hands-on experience and are awarded DIT certificates on completion of their course.

  • A balanced diet

    Students are fed on a balanced diet, prepared on site in the school kitchen and eaten in the dining hall.

  • Highly trained teachers

    Our teachers are highly trained and experienced, and they know the national curriculum inside out.

  • Affordable fees and bursaries

    Affordable school fees, plus bursary programmes from PESS and other organisations for families who need them.

On campus

Facilities

Laboratories

The science laboratory is fully equipped with apparatus and the chemicals to use it, so practicals are done rather than described. Physics, Chemistry and Biology students work at the bench every week, and there is a separate computer laboratory next door.

Library

The library is stocked for both the old and the new curriculum, so no student has to work from borrowed notes. It stays open through the day and into evening prep for the boarding section.

ICT and standby power

ICT is built into the way subjects are taught, not saved up for one lesson a week. A standby generator carries the lessons, the practicals and evening prep straight through every power cut.
